# AI search visibility by Seenby

AI search visibility tracking for your brand. Seenby records brand mentions in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI: where you're missing, who's named instead and which pages they cite. Use it for AI visibility tracking, AI Overviews tracking, LLM brand monitoring and generative engine optimization (GEO).

## Connect with AI assistants

Our [MCP server](https://github.com/seenbyapp/mcp-server) connects Seenby to compatible AI clients, with browser sign-in and explicit permission.

get_account and get_brand_report read your plan, tracked brands and the action list; get_question_history and list_cited_pages read answer history and cited pages; suggest_setup, create_brand and run_brand_now set up and run tracking within your plan's limits (runs use your plan's answers; a Free account has 5 checks to try it); add/remove questions and competitors; check_website asks ChatGPT three buyer questions about any website. Everything runs from a Seenby account: a free one comes with 5 checks, no card.
